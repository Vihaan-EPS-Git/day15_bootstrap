This is the Math Course:

Course Syllabus: Algebra 2 (Fall / Spring Semester)
Instructor: Mr. Davis | Email: mdavis@school.edu | Room: 204

📚 Course Description: Algebra 2 builds upon the concepts learned in Algebra 1 and Geometry. This course focuses on advanced algebraic techniques, including the study of functions, polynomials, rational expressions, complex numbers, logarithmic functions, and basic probability and statistics. Emphasis is placed on real-world problem-solving and critical thinking.

🎯 Course Objectives:
• Solve and graph linear, quadratic, polynomial, and exponential functions.
• Manipulate radical and rational expressions with confidence.
• Understand and apply logarithmic properties.
• Analyze statistical data and solve basic probability scenarios.

📅 Weekly Course Outline:
• Weeks 1-3 (Foundations & Linear Systems): Review of linear equations, inequalities, and solving systems with 2 and 3 variables.
• Weeks 4-7 (Quadratic Functions & Complex Numbers): Graphing parabolas, quadratic formula, factoring, and operations with imaginary numbers (i).
• Weeks 8-11 (Polynomial Functions): Polynomial operations, factoring high-degree polynomials, and finding roots.
• Weeks 12-15 (Rational & Radical Expressions): Simplifying rational expressions, solving radical equations, and inverse functions.
• Weeks 16-18 (Exponential & Logarithmic Functions): Exponential growth and decay, properties of logarithms, and solving exponential equations.
• Weeks 19-20 (Final Review & Exams): Comprehensive review of all semester concepts and final assessments.

📝 Grading Policy: Tests & Quizzes: 45% | Homework & Classwork: 25% | Projects & Presentations: 15% | Final Exam: 15%

🎒 Required Materials: Scientific or Graphing Calculator (TI-84 recommended), Dedicated Math Notebook / Binder, Pencils and erasers (all math work must be done in pencil!).
