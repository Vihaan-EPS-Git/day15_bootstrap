This is the Science Course:

Course Syllabus: Biology (Fall / Spring Semester)
Instructor: Dr. Martinez | Email: amartinez@school.edu | Room: 108

📚 Course Description: Biology explores the study of life and living organisms, from the cellular and molecular level up to complex ecosystems. Students will investigate genetics, evolution, ecology, and human anatomy through hands-on laboratory experiments, scientific inquiry, and critical analysis.

🎯 Course Objectives:
• Understand cellular structure, function, and energy processes like photosynthesis and cellular respiration.
• Analyze DNA structure, inheritance patterns, and genetic expression.
• Examine the mechanisms of evolution and natural selection.
• Evaluate ecological interactions and human impacts on biodiversity.

📅 Weekly Course Outline:
• Weeks 1-3 (The Chemistry of Life & Cells): Introduction to biochemistry, cell organelles, and transport mechanisms.
• Weeks 4-7 (Cellular Energy & Division): Photosynthesis, cellular respiration, mitosis, and meiosis.
• Weeks 8-11 (Genetics & Heredity): Mendelian genetics, DNA replication, protein synthesis, and genetic mutations.
• Weeks 12-15 (Evolution & Biodiversity): Natural selection, evidence for evolution, and classification of organisms.
• Weeks 16-18 (Ecology & Ecosystems): Energy flow, nutrient cycles, population dynamics, and environmental conservation.
• Weeks 19-20 (Final Review & Capstone Project): Comprehensive review and student scientific research presentations.

📝 Grading Policy: Tests & Quizzes: 40% | Lab Reports: 30% | Homework & Classwork: 20% | Final Project: 10%

🎒 Required Materials: Dedicated Science Notebook / Lab Journal, Colored Pencils, Scientific Calculator, Safety Goggles (provided in class).
